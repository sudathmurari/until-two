Until Two

The baby's day stays on your phone.

Until Two is a calm log for a baby's first two years. Log a feed, a bottle, a pump, a diaper, or a nap in one or two taps.

  • No account and no server. Until Two uploads nothing from the log. Your phone's own backup includes the log, under your account.
  • Hand over the day. A care file goes to whoever takes the next shift, only when you send it.
  • On your wrist. Apple Watch and Wear OS show the running timer and log a diaper with a tap.

A dad built Until Two after his little one was born.
