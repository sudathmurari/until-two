How do I share the log with my partner?

Settings → Care file → Send a care file. On the other iPhone, open the file with Until Two, or use Settings → Care file → Import a care file. Each import merges the new entries, so you can send it after every shift.

Each care file asks for a password when you send it. Tell it to the other person yourself, not in the same chat as the file. Nothing opens the file without it.

How do I move to a new phone?

If you set up the new phone straight from the old one, or restore it from the old phone's backup, the log comes with it. Otherwise, send a care file to yourself, by AirDrop or to Files, before you set up the new phone. On the new phone, choose Open a care file on the welcome screen.

Can I bring in my log from another app?

Until Two can bring in a CSV export from Huckleberry or Baby Tracker. Export the log from that app, then choose the file in Settings → Care file → Import a care file. Before anything is saved, Until Two shows what it will add and how many rows it could not read. It copies the entries once; nothing stays connected to the other app, and nothing is sent anywhere. Importing the same export again adds nothing new.

Is there a backup?

The phone's own backup includes the log: an iCloud or computer backup on iPhone, the Google backup on Android. Until Two keeps no copy of its own anywhere else. To keep one yourself, save a care file to Files now and then. If Files is set to iCloud Drive, that copy is kept by iCloud under Apple's terms. The privacy policy says how to leave the log out of the phone's backup.

How do I start a timer without opening the app?

Add an Until Two widget to the Home Screen or the Lock Screen, or add its control to Control Center. A running timer shows on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, where you can pause it or switch sides. Stop it from the Timer widget or with Siri. You can also tell Siri, with nothing to set up: "Log a wet diaper in Until Two", "Start nursing in Until Two", "Switch sides in Until Two", "Baby woke up in Until Two", "Stop pumping in Until Two" or "Pause the timer in Until Two". Most other ways of saying it work too, and Siri says what it did.

With twins, how do I tell the timers apart?

With two or more children, each running timer on the Lock Screen, on the widgets and on the Apple Watch shows that child's name. Until Two tells you the first time it happens. To keep names off, turn them off in Settings → This phone; that choice stays when you add or remove a child. Settings → This phone → Hide names on Today and the lock screen hides every name outside the app.

How do I ask a question about the log?

On an iPhone with Apple Intelligence and iOS 26.4 or later, open Insights and type or speak into Ask, or say "Ask Until Two" to Siri. The answer lists the entries it used, so you can check it. Settings → Intelligence shows whether this iPhone can answer. Answers can be wrong, and Ask does not give medical advice.

Accessibility

Until Two follows the phone's appearance: light when the phone is light, and Night black when the phone is dark. To keep one look, choose Light, Dark or Night black in Settings → This phone → Appearance. Text keeps at least WCAG AA contrast in every theme. Animation stops when Reduce Motion is on. Every activity is named in words, not only by its colour. If something is hard to use with VoiceOver, larger text, or anything else, please write to the address above.

Does it show ads?

No. A dad built Until Two after his little one was born, and nothing in it is an ad.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Delete the log, then type DELETE. Removing the app also erases the log.

Does Until Two give medical advice?

No. It records what you log. Growth charts name their source. For a concern about your baby, call your pediatrician.
